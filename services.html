Services

Practice areas

Documentation-focused legal work for property, banking, tenancy, and estate matters — prepared with care and licensed under the Bar Council.

01

Sale & Purchase Agreements

We prepare sale and purchase agreements for property purchasers and vendors, covering the documentation involved in a property transaction.

Typically for: purchasers, vendors, developers.

02

Bank Loan Documentation

We prepare bank loan documents in connection with property financing, working alongside the relevant bank's requirements.

Typically for: purchasers, banks.

03

Tenancy Agreements

We prepare tenancy agreements setting out the terms between landlord and tenant.

Typically for: landlords, tenants.

04

Probate & Letters of Administration

We prepare Letters of Administration and probate documentation for the administration of a deceased's estate.

Typically for: deceased's estates and their representatives.

Scope of engagement
